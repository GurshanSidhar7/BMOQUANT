What does the summary mean? Price change compares the first and last retained closes inside your selection. Average volume is the mean shares traded per observed row in that selection. Recent volatility uses the latest row’s stored 20-return measure.

Earlier history is preserved. Returns and rolling measures were calculated in Python before these date filters. They can use observations before your selected start. Blank volatility means there was not enough history. These are unadjusted price measures.
